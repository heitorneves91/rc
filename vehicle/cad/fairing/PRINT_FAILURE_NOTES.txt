NOSE_R_1 / PLATE 4 — FIRST PHYSICAL FAILURE REPORT
Evidence: repository-root failure.jpeg, supplied by the user after printing.

Observation: the curved outer fender has heavily drooping/loose extrusions,
while the adjacent broad panel is substantially smoother and the visible brim
appears attached. Nearby tree supports also have rough/gapped walls. A still
photo cannot establish whether a support broke, when damage started, or whether
supports had already been removed. The user confirmed critical-only supports and adaptive layer height were
enabled. Other job settings and whether supports were removed are unconfirmed.

Leading diagnosis: insufficient effective support beneath the curved fender,
possibly compounded by thin-shell motion, support instability or cooling/speed.
This is an inference, not a confirmed printer/extruder fault. Global clogging or
bed detachment should not be asserted from this image alone.

Important profile discovery: the supplied fairing-P1S.3mf project settings have
support_critical_regions_only=1. The small P1S_PLA_process.json does not override
this inherited setting. Earlier advice describing the original project as a
broader automatic-support baseline was incorrect. A slicer PASS proves slicing
completed, not that a physical overhang will print successfully.
Bambu Studio maintainer explanation: critical-only supports can omit curved
non-critical overhangs and target cantilevers/fully suspended regions:
https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/8402

Suggested controlled retry (not yet printed or validated):
- Print Nose_R alone, retaining the supplied seam-down orientation initially.
- Disable Support critical regions only; retain automatic trees, 35-degree
  threshold and 6 mm brim as the initial baseline.
- Verify support/interface actually follows the underside of the failed fender;
  do not assume two nearby tree trunks provide continuous adequate support.
  Paint enforcement where necessary. Bed-only support must not prevent reaching
  an underside; inspect the sliced layers.
- Start with fixed 0.20 mm layers to isolate the support change. Revisit adaptive
  layers after a successful print; they cannot replace support under a curve.
- Conservative trial speeds: outer walls about 60 mm/s, troublesome overhangs
  about 20-30 mm/s. Keep PLA part cooling active. Verify supports remain stable.
- Use the actual filament profile. Do not blindly change flow, nozzle temperature
  or infill to address a localized unsupported surface.
- Check the earliest damaged layers in Preview; unsupported extrusion paths,
  sparse contact, broken support or local shell wobble suggest different fixes.

Do not mark a retry successful or revise the print-kit mass/time until re-sliced
and physically tested. The currently distributed package is unchanged.

FOLLOW-UP: Revision 3 replaces these wraparound shells with separate hood/deck,
fender and cabin panels. Read FAIRING.txt for the current geometry and print
settings; the retry suggestions above refer to the old revision-2 nose. Most
joints have integrated glue laps with tapered roots. The two printed wing/bumper
parts are locked and excluded from the remaining print queue.
