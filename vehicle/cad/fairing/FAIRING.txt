RC BUGGY FAIRING — REVISION 3: SEPARATE SURFACES / LOWER SUPPORT

USER REQUIREMENTS AND REFERENCE
The reference is fairing.png at the repository root. Revision 3 keeps the lower, curved, lightweight
revision-2 envelope and replaces the large wraparound shell sections with
independently oriented panels and integrated recessed glue laps. The requested corrections are:
- Redesign shell splits for lower support, preserving the already printed rear
  wing and front bumper exactly.
- Two rear lamp housings/signatures like the reference; no rear grille.
- Lower the whole body. Extra servo-saver accommodation is lateral, not an
  excuse to raise the hood, skirt or roof.
- The accepted assembly pose is already maximum suspension compression.
  Reserve downward travel, not another 15 mm upward stroke.
- Controlled curved surfaces and blended character lines, rather than boxes
  or uncontrolled wavy lofts.
- Thin cosmetic skins, two print walls and 5% infill, with less plastic.

Print everything in white PLA if desired; lamp details can be painted later.
Windows remain open, as on the detached body in the reference. Lights are
cosmetic geometry, not electrical lamp holders. This is a dimensionally adapted
interpretation of the image; the image contains no manufacturing dimensions.

FILES AND REBUILD
../fairing.FCStd is the separate body document. Its Fairing group contains the
physical parts in the global car coordinates. PrintOrientations contains
hidden, bed-oriented copies; show it alone. ClearanceReferences contains hidden
non-printable saver and suspension envelopes. ../full_assembly.FCStd embeds
the physical fairing under its Fairing group. No joints/live CAD links are used.
The 74 accepted mechanical components remain unchanged.

build_fairing.py is the editable design source. Change its P dictionary and
geometry functions, then run ../rebuild_fairing.FCMacro in FreeCAD. The macro
replaces only the fairing snapshots in the assembly. Close the separate Fairing
document first. parameters.json is a generated audit snapshot, not a second
input file. CAD metadata is explanatory: changing a text property alone does
not regenerate surfaces or STLs.

../rebuild_assembly.FCMacro reinstalls the existing fairing.FCStd when present,
but does not redesign it. After changing mechanical placements, rebuild the
fairing so its clearance references use the new wheel/pivot coordinates.
Run ../verify_fairing.FCMacro after a rebuild. verification.json must say PASS.
Precise minimum-distance searches are optional; verify.main(True) enables them.
The required checks always use exact intersections and explicit clearance tools.
render_fairing.py produces assembled, rear, isolated and exploded CAD previews.

RESOURCE USE / CRASH RECOVERY
Use a separate FreeCAD Python process restricted to two CPU cores, lower
priority and roughly 4 GiB of process address space for heavy CAD work on this
machine. A desktop crash occurred during development; saved scripts survived,
while /tmp did not. Do not depend on /tmp caches for reproducibility.
The repository includes run_headless.py for this bounded workflow. In a separate
FreeCAD AppImage Python process, use:
  <extracted FreeCAD>/AppRun python <repo>/vehicle/cad/fairing/run_headless.py build
Use verify or render as the final argument for those stages. Do NOT execute
this launcher inside an existing interactive FreeCAD console: its resource
limits are intended for a disposable subprocess, not the whole user's GUI.
The generator avoids combining dozens of overlapping tires into one Boolean.
Steering-angle capsules remain separate and are cut serially. Do not revert to
a huge multiFuse of the entire steering/travel sweep. The same rule applies to
verification: check individual overlapping capsules, not a single compound cut.
Save source/working checkpoints before an expensive rebuild. Do not interpret
an unfinished build or an old PASS report as verification of new meshes.

SURFACES, HEIGHT AND THICKNESS
Coordinates: X across the car, +Y forward, +Z upward. Left is negative X.
The existing assembly tire centers are Y=-32.5/-301, Z=28 at full compression.
The nominal ground plane of that compressed pose is Z=-16.5. At normal ride
height the wheels move downward relative to the chassis; do not mistake this
compressed visualization for the normal ride height.

The skirt is Z=3..11 rather than the former 18..26. Body mounting pads meet the
shorter brackets at Z=11 instead of Z=26. Roof design stations are Z=116..118
rather than 148. Hood and rear deck are lowered too. The existing camera/ESP32
support still requires real clearance: its accepted highest point is Z=102.4.
That hardware, not a vertical saver allowance, sets the minimum useful roof.
Revision 3 trims only the free cabin skirt to the existing mounting-pad plane
at Z=11 for flat bed contact; it does not lift the body or alter the pad/bolt
interfaces. Front/tail contours retain their low skirts.
Rear contours accommodate the actual shock tower locally; do not raise the
entire body to resolve a local clash.

The shell uses nominal 1.0 mm profile skins. Only screw pads, tabs and brackets
are thicker. The wing plane is 1.4 mm with a thin rear lip/strakes; endplates
are 1.2 mm. Do not attempt to save weight only by lowering infill: shell volume
and support dominate this cosmetic body. CAD solid-mass estimates and actual
slicer totals are separate values in the verification/slicer reports.

Surface construction is explicit tensor B-spline patches: rounded quadratic
Bezier cross-sections and shape-preserving cubic Hermite interpolation along
Y. The Y parameter is fixed to the actual station coordinates. This avoids
uncontrolled global-loft dips, ripples and mismatched inner/outer parameterization.
The broad molded panels remain smooth while the intended styling edges remain
defined. Keep the interpolation shape-preserving when changing stations.
Nominal thickness is set in the X/Z profile; normal thickness on sloped surfaces
may differ. Every physical solid and every final STL is checked.

SUSPENSION / STEERING
Current assembly position is maximum compression. Revision 2 uses a continuous
20 mm downward allowance and only 3 mm residual upward tolerance, because the
user said the current pose could be a few millimetres below the exact stop.
It must not use the old +/-15 mm travel assumption.

Static arch capsules use 48.5 mm radius, extending from center Z=8 to Z=31,
starting at X=+/-92. They cover the downward range and leave room around the
44.5 mm source tires. Front motion uses 47.5 mm-radius, 42 mm-long inflated
tires, steered about the actual knuckle axis from -30 to +30 degrees in 5-degree
steps, each extended continuously through the same vertical travel. The wheel
center is offset from the steering pivot: do not rotate it about its own center.
Keep the angle tools separate for stable CAD computation.

These are explicit clearance assumptions, not solved articulated wishbone
trajectories. Camber/track may also change on the physical car. Recheck actual
travel and the final saver/steering limit when those are measured. Do not change
the accepted suspension placements just to make the fairing fit.

PRINT KIT AND ORIENTATION — REVISION 3
Why: the first physical Nose_R_1 print failed on its curved outer fender after
critical-only supports and adaptive layers were enabled. See failure.jpeg at
the repository root and PRINT_FAILURE_NOTES.txt. A hood + fender + deep skirt
in one part forced support-heavy compromises; changing the split is preferable
to accepting a support forest or unsupported exterior curves.

The exterior B-spline surfaces, overall height and clearances remain as in
revision 2. The transverse body junctions remain Y=-90/-246, with 0.30 mm gaps.
New shell splits (mirrored left/right):
FreeCAD rotation +90 degrees around X makes +Y the upward build direction.
-90 degrees makes -Y upward. Check the actual first layers; labels alone do
not prove the intended end is on the bed. The verifier checks these directions
and requires substantial bed contact beneath each cabin sill.
- Nose_hood: X=0.15..84.85, full nose length. Print center seam edge down.
- Nose_fender_aft: X>=85.15, Y=-89.85..-32.65. Rear cut edge down, build forward.
- Nose_fender_fore: X>=85.15, Y=-32.35..9.85. Forward cut edge down, build aft.
- Nose_cheek: X>=85.15, Y>=10.15. Rear cut edge down, build forward.
- Tail_deck: X=0.15..84.85. Center seam edge down.
- Tail_fender_aft: X>=85.15, Y<=-301.15. Rear end down, build forward.
- Tail_fender_fore: X>=85.15, Y>=-300.85. Front end down, build aft.
- Cab_sill: Z=11..69.85, with unchanged chassis mounting pads. The free skirt
  is trimmed to the existing pad underside at Z=11: pads and the complete sill
  start on the bed together, instead of on tiny curved skirt tips.
- Cab_frame: Z=70.15..104.85. Roof cut edge down, making a short inverted frame
  instead of a 156 mm tall window surround.
The hood/deck panels are mostly vertical in their build orientation. Separate
fenders build from their broad transverse ends toward the wheel crown, so the
arch removes material as printing progresses rather than creating a long
unsupported roof above an empty arch.

Recessed, integrated glue laps replace a pile of separate new splice parts.
Their roots taper over 4 mm in the build direction, so a recessed lap does not
first appear as a floating island that creates its own tall support tower.
Nominal glue allowance is 0.4 mm, lap thickness 0.8 mm; local webs connect each
lap to its owner panel only. Hood/deck laps extend under the separate fenders;
aft fender laps bridge the wheel-center split; a separate shallow
Join_Nose_cheek strip bridges the Y=10 seam so both panels keep their broad
transverse edge on the bed; sill laps support the front/rear pillars and B-pillar of the frame.
Do not sand these laps flush or force them into zero-clearance contact. Dry-fit
and use PLA-compatible adhesive. There is one small separate cheek joiner per side, not a new joiner at every
seam. The original transverse/centerline backing
strips remain in the kit for joining the assembled nose/cab/tail sections. Rear
transverse backing now stops at Z=61, below the new pillar lap at Z=63; do not
restore its old height or it will overlap the sill lap.

VERIFICATION AND FUTURE AGENTS
Preserve the accepted 74 mechanical components, including rear shocks behind
the same chassis ears, front shocks on lower-arm centers, rear shocks on aft
lower-arm ends, correct ball-socket directions and aligned servo mounts. No
chassis locks. No new joints, solver proxies or cross-document dependency loops.

Required after any geometry change:
- Rebuild the separate CAD, installed assembly snapshots and every STL.
- Archive/remove obsolete generated STLs; do not include stale parts in the kit.
- Verify one valid solid per physical component, watertight meshes, grounded
  orientations, P1S bounds and mesh/CAD volume agreement.
- Verify exact intersections against all mechanics, between printed parts,
  against the lateral saver reserve and against every individual motion capsule.
- Keep full_assembly's 74 mechanical identities/placements unchanged.
- Re-render native CAD, including rear view, and visually inspect the surfaces.
- Verify locked wing/bumper B-reps and mesh hashes; never change their interfaces.
- Re-slice all remaining final parts at two walls / 5% infill. Update model-only 3MF,
  print manifest, slicer report, checksums and fairing-print.zip together.
- Update these instructions and vehicle/AGENTS.md when assumptions change.

Never rely on a previous PASS report or old mass estimate after editing shapes.
Use optimalBoundingBox() / mesh bounds for trimmed surfaces. Do not fix a
collision by ignoring the verifier or by adding unnecessary whole-body height.
